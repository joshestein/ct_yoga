# Yoga Schedule Dashboard

One page that shows the Upcoming Yoga Classes at the Cape Town Studios I attend.

## Glossary

**Studio**
A yoga business with a physical place where classes happen.

**Booking Platform**
The third-party system where a Studio publishes its schedule and takes bookings. It is not always the Studio's website builder.

**Yoga Class**
One in-studio yoga session at one Studio and one time. "Slow Flow on Tuesday at 18:00" is one Yoga Class. Sound baths, breathwork, sauna, studio hire, and online classes are not Yoga Classes.

**Upcoming**
Starts in the next 7 days.

**Full**
No spots left. A Yoga Class with only a waitlist is Full.

**Booking Link**
A URL that opens the Yoga Class on its Booking Platform. If the platform has no such URL, it opens the Studio's schedule.

**Snapshot**
The Yoga Classes collected from one Studio at one time. It can be out of date. The Booking Platform is the source of truth.
